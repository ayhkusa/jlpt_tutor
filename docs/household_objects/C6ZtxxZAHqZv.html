鍋敷き : なべしき

pot coaster

Japanese sentence
熱い鍋を鍋敷きの上に置いて、テーブルを守ります。
English sentence
I place the hot pot on a pot coaster to protect the table.
Sentence breakdown
JapanesePronounceMeaningFunction
熱い鍋をhot potを particle: marks the object placed
鍋敷きの上にon the pot coasterの links 鍋敷き to 上; に marks the location
置いてplace andて-form: links placing with protecting
テーブルをthe tableを particle: marks the object protected
守りますprotectpolite verb; the subject 'I' is omitted
