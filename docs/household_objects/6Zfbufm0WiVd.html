テーブル : てーぶる

table

Japanese sentence
家族とテーブルで晩ご飯を食べます。
English sentence
I eat dinner at the table with my family.
Sentence breakdown
JapanesePronounceMeaningFunction
家族とwith my familyと particle: companion; 'with' a person
テーブルでat the tableで particle: place where an action occurs
晩ご飯をdinnerを particle: marks the direct object of 食べます
食べますeatpolite verb; Japanese normally omits the subject when context is clear
Grammar noteと marks the companion, で marks the place, and を marks the food eatenlearner grammar guidance
