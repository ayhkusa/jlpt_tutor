排水口 : はいすいこう

drain

Japanese sentence
排水口にごみがたまったので、きれいに掃除します。
English sentence
Trash collected in the drain, so I clean it thoroughly.
Sentence breakdown
JapanesePronounceMeaningFunction
排水口にin the drainに particle: marks the location where trash collected
ごみがtrashが particle: marks the subject of たまった
たまったのでbecause it collectedので: gives a reason; たまった is the plain past form before it
きれいにcleanlyadverb
掃除しますcleanpolite verb; the subject 'I' and object 'it' are omitted
