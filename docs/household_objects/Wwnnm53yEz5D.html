時計台 : とけいだい

clock stand

Japanese sentence
時計台を玄関に置いて、出かける時間を見ます。
English sentence
I put the clock stand in the entryway and check the time to leave.
Sentence breakdown
JapanesePronounceMeaningFunction
時計台をclock standを particle: marks the object placed
玄関にin the entrywayに particle: marks the destination
置いてput andて-form: links placing with checking
出かける時間をtime to leave出かける modifies 時間; を marks the time checked
見ますlook atpolite verb; the subject 'I' is omitted
Grammar noteA plain-form verb can modify a noun: 出かける時間 means 'the time to leave'learner grammar guidance
