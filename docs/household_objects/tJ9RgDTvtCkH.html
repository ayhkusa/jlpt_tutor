衣類圧縮袋 : いるいあっしゅくぶくろ

clothing compression bag

Japanese sentence
厚い布団を衣類圧縮袋に入れて、場所を取らなくします。
English sentence
I put the thick futon in a compression bag to make it take less space.
Sentence breakdown

入れてput in andて-form: links putting in with reducing space
