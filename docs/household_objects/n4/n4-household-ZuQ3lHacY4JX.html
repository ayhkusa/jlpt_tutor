花瓶敷き : かびんしき

vase coaster

Japanese sentence
花瓶敷きを敷いて、その上に花びんを置きます。

English sentence
I place a vase coaster and put the vase on it.
Sentence breakdown
JapanesePronounceMeaningFunction
花瓶敷きをvase coasterを particle: marks the coaster spread
敷いてspread andて-form: links spreading with placing
その上にon itに particle: marks the location; 上 means 'top'
花びんをvaseを particle: marks the object placed
置きますplacepolite verb; the subject 'I' is omitted
Grammar noteその上に means 'on top of it'; the て-form links laying down the coaster with placing the vaselearner grammar guidance
