衣類乾燥機 : いるいかんそうき

clothes dryer

Japanese sentence
雨が続くときは、衣類乾燥機で服を乾かします。

English sentence
When the rain continues, I dry clothes in the clothes dryer.
Sentence breakdown
JapanesePronounceMeaningFunction
雨が続くときはwhen the rain continuesとき means 'when'; は marks the time situation as the topic
衣類乾燥機でin the clothes dryerで particle: marks the appliance used
