浴槽 : よくそう

bathtub

Japanese sentence
浴槽にお湯をためて、夜にゆっくり入ります。

English sentence
I fill the bathtub with hot water and take a relaxing bath at night.
Sentence breakdown
JapanesePronounceMeaningFunction
浴槽にin the bathtubに particle: marks the container receiving water
お湯をhot waterを particle: marks the water added
ためてfill andて-form: links filling with bathing
夜にat nightに particle: marks the time
ゆっくり入りますtake a relaxing bathゆっくり is an adverb; polite verb phrase
Grammar noteに marks both the destination of the water and the time of the bathlearner grammar guidance
