額縁 : がくぶち

picture frame

Japanese sentence
額縁に絵を入れて、リビングの壁にかけます。
English sentence
I put a picture in a frame and hang it on the living-room wall.
Sentence breakdown
JapanesePronounceMeaningFunction
額縁にin the picture frameに particle: marks the frame receiving the picture
絵をpictureを particle: marks the picture put in
入れてput in andて-form: links framing with hanging
リビングの壁にon the living-room wallの links リビング to 壁; に marks the destination
かけますhangpolite verb; the subject 'I' is omitted
Grammar noteに marks both the frame and the wall as destinations, while の forms 'living-room wall'learner grammar guidance
