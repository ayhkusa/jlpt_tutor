アルミホイル : あるみほいる

aluminum foil

Japanese sentence
魚をアルミホイルで包んで、オーブンで焼きます。
English sentence
I wrap the fish in aluminum foil and bake it in the oven.
Sentence breakdown
JapanesePronounceMeaningFunction
魚をfishを particle: direct object of 包んで
アルミホイルでwith aluminum foilで particle: material/tool used for wrapping
包んでwrap andて-form: connects the preparation action to the main action
オーブンでin the ovenで particle: place/appliance where 焼きます occurs
焼きますbakepolite verb; omitted object is the wrapped fish
Grammar noteで marks both the foil as material and the oven as the appliance; て-form links wrapping and bakinglearner grammar guidance
